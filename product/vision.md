# Product vision

Kampaku is not another chat interface. It is a personal harness for persistent, autonomous AI agents that understands a person's Projects, accepts high-level Goals, transforms them into governed work, and keeps progressing across machines and model providers.

The guiding relationship is: **the person defines intent and priorities; Kampaku manages execution while preserving visibility and control.**

## Product principles

- durable work over ephemeral conversation;
- autonomy bounded by explicit authority;
- inspectable plans and auditable actions;
- provider and worker portability;
- graceful interruption, resumption, and correction;
- private projects remain private runtime data.
